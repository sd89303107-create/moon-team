moon-team n8n

這是林昱凱個人使用的自動化工具，在他自己的電腦上執行 n8n，用來讀寫他本人的 Google 試算表和雲端硬碟，不對外提供服務。

This is a personal automation tool used only by its owner. It runs n8n on the owner's own computer to read and write the owner's Google Sheets and Drive files. It is not offered to anyone else.

隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

  • 只存取擁有者本人授權的 Google 帳號資料（Sheets、Drive）。
  • 資料只存放在擁有者的電腦和他自己的 Google 帳號，不出售、不分享給第三方，也不拿來投放廣告。
  • 擁有者隨時可以到 Google 帳戶的「第三方存取權」撤銷授權。
  • Only the owner's own Google data (Sheets, Drive) is accessed, with the owner's consent. Data stays on the owner's computer and Google account. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for ads. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access page.
